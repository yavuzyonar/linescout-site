About OddsLighthouse

Last updated August 2026

OddsLighthouse is an independent publisher covering legal sports betting and online casino gaming in the United States. We write plain-language explainers, bankroll and strategy guides, and honest reviews of licensed operators, aimed at people who are new to betting or want a clearer picture of how it actually works.

How we make money

OddsLighthouse is supported by affiliate partnerships: when a reader signs up with a sportsbook or casino through a link on this site, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to the reader. This never affects the facts we report — our house rule is that a page's advice and ratings are written before we decide which operators to link, not the other way around. Full detail is in our advertising disclosure.

Editorial standards

  • We date every article and update it when odds, offers, or state rules change.
  • We link to primary sources (state gaming commissions, operator terms) wherever we cite a specific rule or number.
  • We do not promote unlicensed or offshore operators.
  • Every page includes responsible-gambling resources, not just a footer disclaimer.
